> Draft — publish after owner review. Technical practice notes, not legal advice.

1. **Third-party resources**: Google Fonts, external CDNs, maps and video embeds send visitor IPs to third parties. Self-host where you can.
2. **Cookies and analytics**: prefer cookie-less analytics that does not store full IPs; otherwise use a compliant consent banner.
3. **Forms**: collect only what you need, state where data is stored and for how long, and ask for clear consent.
4. **Legal pages**: privacy policy and legal notice (Impressum) reachable from every page, reviewed by a lawyer.
5. **Storage location**: disclose storage outside the EU and assess whether extra safeguards are needed.
6. **Logs and retention**: server logs contain IPs; set and enforce retention.
7. **Security baseline**: HTTPS, Content Security Policy, timely updates, least-privilege access.
8. **Languages and accessibility**: native-level German and English; basic accessibility.
9. **Vendor contracts**: data processing agreements with hosting, email and CRM providers.
10. **Ongoing checks**: review third-party requests quarterly.
